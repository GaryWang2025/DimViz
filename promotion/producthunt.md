## Product Name:
DimViz — Dimension Visualizer

## Tagline (max 60 chars):
Visualize 1D to 4D math spaces in your browser

## Description:

DimViz is a browser-based mathematical visualization tool that brings abstract mathematical spaces to life — from 1D number lines to 4D hypercubes (tesseracts).

### 🎯 Why DimViz?

Understanding high-dimensional spaces is hard. Textbooks give you formulas, but DimViz gives you **intuition**:

- **1D**: Slide points along a number line, understand basis & norm
- **2D**: Watch vector projections, linear transformations happen in real-time
- **3D**: Rotate a unit sphere, visualize cross products & eigenvalues
- **4D**: Experience a **tesseract** (4D hypercube) projected to 3D, with the 4th dimension encoded as color

### ✨ Key Features:

- 🌀 **4D Tesseract Visualization** — Perspective projection + color gradient encoding
- 🎬 **Auto Demo Mode** — Watch dimensions unfold with smooth animations
- 🔄 **Interactive Rotation** — Drag to rotate, scroll to zoom (powered by Three.js)
- 📝 **Live KaTeX Formulas** — Mathematical definitions rendered in real-time
- ☀️ **Light Theme** — High contrast, easy on the eyes
- 📦 **Zero Dependencies** — Pure HTML/CSS/JS, double-click to open

### 🛠️ Tech Stack:

- Three.js r128 (3D rendering)
- KaTeX (LaTeX formula rendering)
- No build step required

### 🌟 Use Cases:

- **Students**: Build intuition for linear algebra concepts
- **Teachers**: Demonstrate dimensional concepts in class
- **Researchers**: Explore high-dimensional geometry
- **Curious minds**: See what a 4D hypercube looks like!
